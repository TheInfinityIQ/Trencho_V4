# R27 Explosives and exclusive F05 recipe selection

Scope: [Designer assignment](https://github.com/TheInfinityIQ/Trencho_V4/issues/12#issuecomment-6445838034), 27 October 2026. Accepted base `e6548826b1993bd0b4db8538b938a8a8a7e286aa`. This implements A07's manufacture portion and the necessary R02/R27 selection in one existing F05. GDD 6.3, 8 and 23.6 govern values and accounting. Later ammunition/engineering chains, construction, full G04/A05/M0 and Windows native verification remain separate.

## Physical production

R27 consumes exactly ten local owned available M03 Gunpowder and two M01 Metal once, after reserving the complete 1 kg output. Forty operator-work produces one physical M06 Explosives at 1 kg in `munitions_output`. Two present ordinary operators take twenty active seconds; four take ten. F05 retains four positions, 1000 kg input/output, real exclusive reporting/hauling and the common rear clock. Missing, other-reserved, foreign, remote, personal or carried ingredients cannot be consumed; even one missing unit prevents the whole start. M06 remains unavailable until legal physical completion and is never purchased or granted by recipe selection.

The accepted guard/interruption contract remains: full space before consumption; atomic refusal of edits invalidating any active promise; access, staff or pause retains receipt/work/promise; fully earned blocked completion requires no extra work charge when legal; Cancel permanently loses consumed inputs and releases only its own unused promise. Existing physical E02 and M06 share actual output capacity and survive changes/cancellation. Independent R01/F04 and R26/F13 keep distinct people/materials/promises on that same clock.

R27 uses eligible deposited A and distinct planned C under GDD23.6 and the accepted current-route clarification. Other-use claims, foreign/cargo/person/inaccessible/filter-incompatible/pile holdings do not become usable stock. Internal source claim -> carry -> legal deposit counts A -> C -> A once; current valid reroutes replace only their own coverage, and cancelled/pile portions have no C until lawful deposit. An active valid R27 yield contributes one planned M06, never usable inventory. E02 cannot satisfy M06 targets or vice versa. Target/mode reductions retain a consumed batch; whole-yield completion never produces a fractional M06.

## One facility, confirmed changes

The existing F05 owner in `r02_production.gd` extends the fixed lifecycle and retains R02 as its constructor default/public defaults. Only R02 and R27 can be selected. The rear still attaches exactly one F05 activity, not parallel round/explosives owners. Four fixed specifications now exist, while the three facility owners and ledger/haul/storage responsibilities stay explicit.

A request to change an active consumed batch creates a token bound to the selected recipe and exact batch ID. The dialog identifies the old batch and actual consumed receipt, explains permanent loss without refund, own-promise release and retained finished goods. Merely requesting, declining or dismissing changes no recipe, batch, work, receipt, inventory or promise. The modal is an embedded engine dialog and fits both review window sizes. The underlying feedback uses a short pending message rather than repeating the full warning, preserving small-window inspection space.

Confirming consumes the token once, revalidates the selected recipe and batch identity, cancels that batch and selects the other released recipe. Fully earned but physically blocked batches require the same confirmation. A stale request after completion, another start or another selection refuses cancellation and requires a fresh request; superseded/repeated tokens do nothing. A change with no active consumed batch needs no cancellation confirmation and costs no material. Any subsequent start still requires actual current local ingredients, labour, access and full space.

Each recipe retains mode, whole nonnegative target, enabled/pause choice, completed/cancelled history and identity sequence across switches. Inactive records have no active consumed batch, perform no work and own no output forecast. Returning to a recipe restores its choices/history without refunding inputs or reusing batch/output IDs. New recipes default to Continuous. Selection does not change people or create freight.

## Bounded review

Normal main launch retains G03 default and exposes **G04 R27 Explosives**, a declared independent F05 setup with five ordinary available people and exactly four M01 plus twelve M03 at the depot. R02 is selected initially. No refill/reset-live-facility control or initial M06/E02 stock is provided. The existing R02 review keeps its original four people and one metal/one powder; R01/R26 fixtures keep their accepted goods.

Use Select R27 and Operators 2, then +300s: reporting finishes but local inputs are missing. Set Stock target/minimum 1, select R02 without a consumed batch, pause it and assign Hauling 1. Another +300s allows the real pickup/walk/drop jobs to deposit both types. Resume R02 and +10s earns 20/40 work. Select R27: Keep current recipe or Escape dismisses the confirmation without loss; a fresh request plus Confirm change cancels that exact consumed R02 batch, permanently losing its one metal/one powder, and starts R27 only from the remaining real ingredients. R27 retains its target 1. The new receipt is ten powder/two metal; cumulative consumption is eleven powder/three metal, with one of each remaining.

After +10s, R27 has 20/40 work. Try 0 kg and Reject M06 filter are refused while its promise is live. Block output and advance time: no extra work or M06. Restore and supply the remaining ten seconds: exactly one M06/1 kg, promise zero, A=1/C=0, target satisfied. Small-window inspection remains reachable by actual wheel input. These fixture time buttons are deterministic diagnostics, not an accepted playable spatial base.
