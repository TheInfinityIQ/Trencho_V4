## Eventual production contract

- Validate recipe access, eligible completed facility, actual present operators, complete **local unreserved** inputs and full output capacity before starting. Reserve the output and commit the complete input batch together; failure leaves inputs, claims, work and output unchanged. Goods owned remotely, purchased/in transit, aboard a supplier, carried toward the bay or reserved for a shipment/another batch cannot be consumed. Reservations never create a second inventory.
- Accumulate operator-work from actual eligible present people over elapsed simulation intervals. Reassignment, reporting, pause and zero-staff boundaries retain completed work and stop only the affected work; no person contributes twice. Shared pause stops the common simulation. A coarse time advance must produce the same result as equivalent partitioned advances through staff/handling/completion boundaries.
- Finish once at the required work, convert the reserved output capacity into the exact physical output, and never re-consume or re-create it on repeated completion/refresh. Finished output stays in its real bay if its demand disappears; any later delivery remains real hauling. Output-full refuses a new batch before input loss. Other jobs cannot occupy a capacity promise already held for an active batch.
- One selected recipe per facility runs continuously or toward a player stock target. Shipment-reserved rear goods do not satisfy an **unreserved** target. Keep stock-target scope/batch-boundary semantics explicit before implementing that mode; do not silently replace it with a local output counter. Pause retains progress. Changing an active recipe requires player confirmation, cancels its batch, loses already-consumed inputs and resets work. Cancelling an unstarted request costs no material; repeated cancellation releases only its own unused commitments once. Cancelling after start does not refund consumed input or erase prior finished output.
- Apply all **29 active recipes, 11 material resources and 22 active equipment types** directly from the canonical catalogues, including exact yields, masses, facility and access. R09/R10/R22/R23 and E09/E10/E22/E23 remain retired, not aliases that create active stock. Recipe facility names resolve to existing IDs: Workshop F04; Munitions Workshop F05; Support Workshop F09; Vehicle Works F10; Building Materials Plant F13. Preserve their staff/input/output limits from GDD 8.1 rather than inventing generic unlimited bays.
- F10's output value zero means **one heavy object/vehicle bay**, not free or unlimited storage. A produced hull has no crew, passengers, fuel or ammunition; integral mount mechanisms do not create spare loose weapons. New weapons are empty. Vehicle batches require the prescribed appropriate fleet admission and legal parking/dispatch destination before start, separately from the heavy bay. D03 admissions do not create people or physical parking. A completed hull without lawful actual qualified driver clearance blocks the bay with its reason; no remote move or fleet-slot refund may unblock it.

## Integration, state and unresolved decisions

Reuse G01's actual holders, unique identities, available/reserved quantities and explicit consumption/creation audit. The future production owner retains selected recipe/mode/target, batch identity, exact input-consumption receipt, output reservation, required/completed work, pause/cancel/completion state and blocking cause. G03 remains responsible for actual reporting/presence, exclusive duties, hauling source/destination promises and safe handoffs; production adds its output promises to the same physical capacity accounting. G02 unloading remains distinct from onward delivery to F04/F05/F09/F13. Do not sum cached UI totals as inventory or introduce a second clock.

Resolve these genuine details with the Game Designer before the affected implementation, while the first continuous R01 slice remains independently specifiable:

- Which owned rear holders count toward a stock target, and how do active batch output promises/in-flight moves affect deciding to start another whole batch? Exact recipe yields cannot be fractionally reduced; define below-target/overshoot behavior rather than inventing it.
- If an active batch's output bay becomes inaccessible or its capacity/filter is edited, what physical completion/holding behavior is required? Preserve consumed inputs, work, existing goods and the batch's promise; do not silently create overflow inventory or lose a batch.
- Confirm the acceptance seam for produced heavy-object/vehicle clearance across G04 and future G07/G12: GDD requires a real driver, fleet admission and destination, while G03's current yard fixture is bounded to one initialized T01. Do not claim five-hull station/dispatch integration from that fixture, bypass an occupied F10 bay, or waive an existing rule to close G04.

G32 must eventually persist production state together with ledger, staffing/handling and fleet/storage claims, retaining exact IDs, consumed receipts, output promises, work and completion flags. Required restore boundaries include immediately before/after start, partial operator work, zero staff/pause, cancellation and output completion; restore cannot refund/reconsume inputs, reset work or duplicate output. Actual persistence is not implemented by this plan.
