## Eventual production contract

- Validate recipe access, eligible completed facility, actual present operators, complete **local unreserved** inputs and full output capacity before starting. Reserve the output and commit the complete input batch together; failure leaves inputs, claims, work and output unchanged. Goods owned remotely, purchased/in transit, aboard a supplier, carried toward the bay or reserved for a shipment/another batch cannot be consumed. Reservations never create a second inventory.
- Accumulate operator-work from actual eligible present people over elapsed simulation intervals. Reassignment, reporting, pause and zero-staff boundaries retain completed work and stop only the affected work; no person contributes twice. Shared pause stops the common simulation. A coarse time advance must produce the same result as equivalent partitioned advances through staff/handling/completion boundaries.
- Finish once at the required work, convert the reserved output capacity into the exact physical output, and never re-consume or re-create it on repeated completion/refresh. Finished output stays in its real bay if its demand disappears; any later delivery remains real hauling. Output-full refuses a new batch before input loss. Other jobs cannot occupy a capacity promise already held for an active batch.
- One selected recipe per facility runs continuously or toward a player stock target. Shipment-reserved rear goods do not satisfy an **unreserved** target. Use the resolved A/C eligibility and whole-batch minimum-target semantics below; a local output counter is not that target. Pause retains progress. Changing an active recipe requires player confirmation, cancels its batch, loses already-consumed inputs and resets work. Cancelling an unstarted request costs no material; repeated cancellation releases only its own unused commitments once. Cancelling after start does not refund consumed input or erase prior finished output.
- Apply all **29 active recipes, 11 material resources and 22 active equipment types** directly from the canonical catalogues, including exact yields, masses, facility and access. R09/R10/R22/R23 and E09/E10/E22/E23 remain retired, not aliases that create active stock. Recipe facility names resolve to existing IDs: Workshop F04; Munitions Workshop F05; Support Workshop F09; Vehicle Works F10; Building Materials Plant F13. Preserve their staff/input/output limits from GDD 8.1 rather than inventing generic unlimited bays.
- F10's output value zero means **one heavy object/vehicle bay**, not free or unlimited storage. A produced hull has no crew, passengers, fuel or ammunition; integral mount mechanisms do not create spare loose weapons. New weapons are empty. All rear-owned vehicle production orders reserve rear 8 admission; a planned military destination never substitutes for it. Recheck the claim and legal output/collection destination before physical creation, separately from the heavy bay. An accepted Fleet Transfer additionally holds military 24 incoming admission while retaining the rear slot until actual HQ handoff or destruction. D03 admissions do not create people or physical parking. A completed hull without lawful actual qualified driver clearance blocks the bay with its reason; no remote move or fleet-slot refund may unblock it.

### Resolved production boundaries and linked continuations

| Contract | Required boundary / counterexample |

Physical ownership or ledger quantity-minus-reservations alone does not make a carry usable. |
| Separate planned coverage C | Otherwise uncommitted active-batch yields and valid internal-relocation portions between eligible stores suppress duplicate starts, counted once. Invalidated destinations/commitments remove only their own coverage without deleting goods/work or bypassing recovery. C is neither usable inventory nor a reservation of unborn goods. |
| Minimum target / whole batch | When A+C is below target, record one full fixed yield before another start; allow overshoot. A lower target does not cancel a consumed batch. Global coverage never replaces actual local available ingredients, present labour or full output space. |

Other filter edits preserve existing goods and request real relocation. |
| Access / completion | Access loss halfway through work retains consumed inputs/work/output promise and pauses affected work. Restore access and supply the remaining work before creating one empty 4 kg E01. Fully worked but physically invalid completion retains work without goods until legal completion. Repeated completion creates none; overflow, refund/restart and unborn-output piles are forbidden. |
| Hull admission / occupied F10 | All rear-owned manufactured hull orders reserve rear 8, never a planned-military substitute.
