## What runs

F5 opens **G08 Terrain & sight**. The root selector retains unchanged G02 and G01 review scenes. Each selection initializes a separate declared fixture; switching scenes is a reset, not an integrated engagement or persistence operation.

**Movement** offers 124 named cases: all 13 surfaces for a declared person and each T01–T05; slope boundaries and just above for foot/wheel/track; ford depths; actual trunks/boulders/buildings and clear gaps; absent/completed/narrow/incompatible support. Select a case, Start lane, advance explicit seconds, stop at the actual position or reset. Inspection shows actual position/ground height, class, dimensions, facing, base speed, fuel, distance, consumption, local support/depth/classes, planned duration and current support interval. A blocked route reports its physical cell or obstacle and consumes no imaginary traversal fuel.

The person is an explicitly initialized 0.6 × 0.6 m calibration footprint at ordinary battlefield 2 m/s, not a change to production infantry collision, D02's separate 1 m reference square, or rear-hauling speed. Hulls use GDD dimensions, cross-country bases, class, fuel rates and tank capacity. These bodies are declared fixtures without manufactured hulls, people, crew readiness, freight, weapons or combat health. The diagnostic objects are not a second live army ledger. G07 and downstream systems must connect authoritative entities before using these rules in an engagement.

**Sight & cover** offers 38 named declared probe cases. It reports actual ground-relative observer/target heights, role/range in metres, eye and independent ground-muzzle rays, contributing foliage metres/costs, budget, intercept position/cell and blocking cause, and strongest physically valid direct/blast cover with separate declared impact geometry. Observer/target settings are calibration inputs, not hidden enemy information. The target marker is drawn only when the eye observes it. Overview changes only the diagram scale; observer location, ray results and knowledge do not change. There is no omniscient enemy display or full contact/report-age system (G17).

## Physical rules and engineering choices

`TerrainCatalog` data lives in a feature-local script, without a global singleton. `TerrainWorld` owns a small explicit one-metre grid plus actual obstacle/shield volumes. Cells have one dominant surface and separate plane height/gradient, ford and completed-overlay properties. Grades derive from actual gradients. Slope thresholds are inclusive with a 0.00001-degree tolerance for engine Vector2 precision; tests distinguish just-above values at 0.001 degrees. Unconnected height edges are refused rather than teleporting a hull vertically. Authored slopes are continuous planes; a cliff is not a shallow slope merely because both adjacent cell labels are open.

A completed, declared compatible crossing changes support only in its actual cell footprint. A dry road or shallow-swamp/bog-edge causeway also obeys its applicable base/slope restrictions. Unbuilt/incompatible/narrow support cannot bridge a hull's unsupported side, erase an adjacent cliff/tree or remove forbidden slope. Deep bog, ponds and water do not become fords automatically. Marked ford classes, firm bottom, accessible banks and actual depth are all required. The fixture takes compatible support as an explicit input; gross load, build work, bridge admissions, actual approach construction and production crossing validation remain G13/G19. The crossing calibration is level with its declared banks; there is no instant step onto an elevated deck.

Movement uses analytical continuous overlap intervals for a translating oriented rectangular footprint against metre cells and solid prop rectangles. It checks the entire straight lane, including tiny obstacles between endpoints, before admitting it. This is a bounded straight-lane test, not pathfinding or a turning/towing planner: a lane must align with the body's current facing. Footprints are oriented, and rotated-lane regressions exercise that geometry. There is no automatic forest clearing. The diagram is only a review annotation; it is not a production 2D/3D commitment.

At each support interval, speed is the most restrictive **per-cell dominant-terrain × slope** result across the footprint. Distinct surface multipliers never multiply each other. Fuel uses the greatest applicable footprint terrain factor per horizontal metre, with no invented slope burn. T02 on flat road is 15 m/s, not 22.5. This conservative boundary convention keeps a wide hull from receiving full road speed/fuel benefits while partly in mud. Planned legs require actual fuel plus 25% reserve; chronological advancement consumes only travelled metres. Cancellation retains actual pose/distance/fuel; restart plans the remaining lane. Actual exhaustion stops at its affordable position. World revisions revalidate the remaining lane before further movement; a failed recheck stops at the current pose. It does not implement remote terrain-change knowledge (G17), war-time navigation or the complete stranded/recovery scenario.

Sight partitions actual horizontal rays at cell edges and intersects real height planes and opaque 3D box volumes, independently of movement permission. Foliage spends horizontal metres times that surface's cost only in its declared vegetation volume; separated and mixed patches add toward 100, and reaching 100 blocks. Crop height 2.5 m, light canopy 12 m and dense canopy 18 m are explicit calibration geometry, not new fixed production vegetation dimensions. A drone probe at 40 m ignores low crops but cannot reveal people under dense canopy; smoke's declared static opaque test box blocks both roles. That box tests opacity, not mortar delivery or the final circular E24 effect. Ordinary range is 220 m; named ready/active role fixtures use the accepted binocular/driver/technical/APC/tank/lookout/field-gun/drone ranges. Binocular readiness, station staffing and drone launch/controller/battery are declared inputs, not implemented gameplay. Eye and muzzle are separate: a drone or high eye can observe beyond a ridge while the ground rifle muzzle remains blocked. No live shot, damage or accuracy bonus is simulated.

Natural cover applies to dismounted people actually occupying supported, unobstructed cover, using the declared0.6m person footprint. The [Game Designer clarification dated26 October2026](design_decisions.md#natural-blast-cover-clarification--26-october-2026) now makes natural blast classification explicit in GDD14.1: TR06 dense forest gives0.5; TR07 requires a real rock shielding the person from the supplied actual impact origin; all other natural classes, including light forest, swamp and rubble, give1. Missing/nonfinite/coincident impact inputs or unusable rock geometry never fabricate a shield; inspection reports missing/invalid eligibility and no rock benefit. Static fixture impact positions are declared separately as Vector3 map-x/map-y/height inputs; they are not inferred from the observation eye or a camera.

Direct-hit and blast benefits select their own strongest physically valid source independently, never stacking. A bunker can protect from a direct attacker at the front while a rear blast receives only valid dense-forest protection. Inspection shows both winners, candidate lists, actual impact and eligibility. Defined trench0.5/bunker0.25 blast examples remain; actual town-garrison0.5 remains in the canonical design, with garrison gameplay deferred. Roads and exposed completed decks provide no natural cover, and hulls receive no infantry terrain bonus. The review includes all13 natural classes, rock right/wrong/no shield/missing impact, impossible occupied forest and independently directed bunker cases. Static shields are cover probes, not implemented garrison entry/windows/occupancy or damage. D02's fairness sampler and final-world A17/A18 remain G10. This clarification resolves the earlier design question; native verification remains a separate gate.

## Ownership, interruption and G32 obligations

`TerrainWorld` owns actual bounds, cell surfaces/plane heights/gradients/depth/ford permissions, overlays/completion/compatibility, solid/opaque obstacle volumes, directional shield geometry and revision. The current declared blast impact belongs to the review probe input, separate from the observer and independent direct attacker. `LaneSimulation` owns the declared body ID/type/class/footprint/facing/pose, real remaining fuel, cumulative distance/burn, clock, active interval/index/elapsed time and planned endpoint. Inspection snapshots are detached. `TerrainDemo` owns only the selected case, latest declared observation and its simple visible/unknown review state; diagram scale owns no simulation facts. Reset is explicit reinitialization, never cargo, fuel or person creation in a live game.

G32 must save actual terrain/props/support state and the authoritative entity/knowledge owners together, not regenerate them from a case name or reroll a world. Preserve pose/facing/footprint, fuel and consumed totals, endpoint and current interval/time, world revision and contact provenance. After restore validate physical support and references before continuing; do not refund burned fuel, reset travelled distance, duplicate movement, snap to an endpoint or infer enemy knowledge from the camera. Required future round trips include mid-interval, immediately before/after fuel exhaustion, stopped/cancelled motion, changed support before revalidation and eye-clear/muzzle-blocked observations. Full persistence remains G32; live causeway loss, recovery/towing, repair not curing terrain stranding and health/crew/stock integration remain their owning issues.

## Sources and rationale

Accepted numbers and scope come from the local canonical GDD, the linked assignment and [D02 contract](d02_cover_fairness.md); no external source overrides those design values. The bounded analytical model, local ownership and conservative boundary convention are engineering choices for inspectable foundations, not performance or production architecture claims. The review uses event-driven [Godot signals](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/getting_started/step_by_step/signals.html) and a diagnostic Control with cached drawing refreshed on changes through [queue_redraw](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/2d/custom_drawing_in_2d.html). There is no per-frame simulation polling or node per metre tile.
