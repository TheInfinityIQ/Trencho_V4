# G19 field structures and roads

This implementation follows [GDD §§13, 14 and 16](game_design_document.md), issue [#27](https://github.com/TheInfinityIQ/Trencho_V4/issues/27), and the Designer's [G19 staging decision](https://github.com/TheInfinityIQ/Trencho_V4/issues/27#issuecomment-6515222949). G20 owns built bridges and the remaining full A23 crossing integration.

## Ownership and physical handoff

`game/field_structures/field_service.gd` owns S01–S12 projects, assigned engineers, service staff, occupants, patients, depot berths, repairs, clearance work, and inspectable blockers. G01 owns actual people, carriers, stock, reservations, consumed quantities, and salvage piles. G08 owns terrain support, overlays, directional cover, and obstacle routing. The rear clock advances construction and repair at event boundaries. G16 still owns wounds and ten-second field care; the aid post supplies a physical place and capacity, not medicine or a free medic.

An order creates one physical site with one-quarter durable health and no operational benefit. Work requires up to four actual local P02 engineers each carrying E08, complete unreserved owned M11 staged at the site, no visible hostile within 150 m, and ten seconds free of site damage. The full M11 batch is consumed once at the first eligible work start. A person or carrier with stock must already be physically within two metres for `deliver_material` to hand it to the site's exact construction supply area. Handing stock over earns no engineer work. A person with an active G01 carry deposits the whole carried load through G01's safe deposit boundary. Remote HQ goods, reservations, and a planning request do not stage anything.

G19 does not prescribe a field travel or unloading rate. G26a owns the timed live forward trip and unloading ETA; G19 accepts its actual arrival endpoint. The A31 regression and review screen use a **controlled carrier-arrival fixture** to exercise that handoff. They do not claim to prove a live HQ-to-field journey. This is the Designer's decision on issue #27, not a substitute pacing rule.

## State transitions and effects

- Paused work retains progress and consumed materials. Cancelling before start leaves all staged stock at the site. Cancelling after start returns half of consumed M11 to a named physical salvage pile and loses the rest; G01 material conservation remains balanced. Destroyed structures need a fresh full blueprint rebuild. Ordinary repairs consume ten local M11 and 40 actual P02 work seconds to restore at most 100 health. A repair point uses one bay and actual P02/E08/M11 for each vehicle or gun repair; it never clears terrain blockage or grants health on request.
- S01 and S03 count actual infantry occupants; bunker ports use construction facing and a 120-degree front arc. At most one actual E05 portable machine gun uses two bunker places. Occupancy grants no weapon, ammunition, crew, or setup. Entry and exit permit one person per second. Enemy occupants block entry; an abandoned shelter may be occupied by either army, while cache goods retain their owner.
- S02 supplies protected-side cover and slows crossing infantry. S04 blocks foot and wheels; tracks can contact it at reduced speed. An actual T05 crushes sound S02/S04 only after three accumulated contact seconds, without free hull damage. A P02/E08 clears wire in 20 work seconds or an S05 vehicle obstacle in 120; S05 blocks wheels and tracks and slows foot movement. Neither action creates materials.
- S06 and S07 hold only physically delivered goods within their 2,000 kg and 10,000 kg limits. S06 is self-service for an actual local carrier; S07 needs two local logistics staff, a stocked depot, and one of two actual truck berths to load. S08 needs a local P03/E11, an admitted actual wounded patient within its eight places, and E12 physically held at the post or by that medic. S10 needs an actual trained local observer with E20 before its 500 m sight can be used. Empty sites provide none of these services.
- S11 becomes a dry, at-most-ten-degree road only at completion and has no health. S12 crosses shallow swamp or bog edge with an 8,000 kg gross-load limit. Its additional 100 M11 and 160 work heavy upgrade closes traffic until completion, then raises the limit to 80,000 kg. Destruction removes the surface, so a wheel vehicle on impassable base mud is Terrain Stranded at the same position and health. Legal base travel by tracks or people remains possible. Rebuilding restores surface without moving or repairing the stranded hull. G13 route planning passes the same actual hull plus its contents and fuel mass to G08's gross-load check.

## Inspection and later persistence

`inspect` exposes site identity, location, code, owner/company, progress, consumed and missing M11, actual assignments, health, occupancy, and blocker. `snapshot` adds detached projects, repairs, clearances, service staff, tank contact, and events. `audit` checks site and engineer links alongside G01 and the other service audits. The review screen at `game/field_structures/field_demo.tscn` allows input and small-window visual inspection of A31's blocked and working states.

G32 must serialize the authoritative field rows, assigned identities, repair/clearance progress, events, world overlays/closure, and G01 physical stock and consumption together at one clock point. Restore must rebuild service indexes and event boundaries without consuming M11 twice or creating a second salvage pile. G32's full save/load round trip remains its own obligation.
