## Behavior and boundaries

The scene-owned `RearSimulation` uses G01's ledger for physical people, ownership, position/holder, exclusive duty, carried stock and source claims. Requests show player-set target, actual catalogue positions, assigned people, reporting/active versus present, shortage and explicit priority. Targets can exceed available people but cannot create a fifth F04 position or a third instructor position. Training staff need completed basic training; driving needs P08. Supplier drivers and away/committed crew do not supply rear labour. Existing fulfilled assignments are retained where compatible; new assignments prefer eligible idle people, then safe rear reassignment, with qualification scarcity and stable IDs resolving otherwise equal choices. People report along actual paths at 1.5 m/s before counting as present.

Staffing competition uses explicit Emergency/High/Normal/Low, greatest unfilled share, then oldest request. Pending safe handoffs occupy their destination target vacancies while remaining actual people in the old duty until completion; inspection separates actual staff from pending arrivals. New arrivals cannot fill the same target vacancy again. The [dated clarification](https://github.com/TheInfinityIQ/Trencho_V4/issues/11#issuecomment-6418512971) keeps staffing priority unchanged until edited by the player. Queued local hauling work alone ages: Low→Normal at 60 simulation seconds, Low→High at 120, Normal→High at 60; High remains High and explicit Emergency remains Emergency. Eligible accessible work chooses effective priority, shortest complete approach-plus-carry distance, then oldest job. Reservation release/retry preserves the original request identity/age. Aging cannot move a person from another pool or interrupt a current carry/driver handoff.

An unmet job warns after 120 seconds of continuous actual eligible contention with explicit Emergency handling. Missing people/access/source/space remain their own reasons; an old shortage does not instantly become Emergency starvation when a new Emergency job appears. Contention tracking resets when the competing cause is absent. All time is simulation time and advances only through explicit fixture buttons; no per-frame scheduler polls the review UI.

Hauling reserves a real source claim and destination space before approach. The reserved amount is whole units, one type per load, at most 25 kg minus retained personal contents. Pickup and putdown each take two seconds; the real carried holder moves at 1.2 m/s. Source, reserved, carried and deposited quantities remain separate. Source access blocked for ten seconds releases only that carry's promises and leaves the original demand; earlier recovery preserves unfinished pickup work/claims. Before pickup, reassignment returns its claims immediately. During carry/drop, the old actual duty remains with one pending destination duty until safe deposit, including repeated requests.

An invalid carry destination chooses the nearest accessible, filter-accepting real storage by actual route distance with sufficient room. With none available, it creates a marked temporary pile at the worker's actual accessible location, then completes real putdown before reassignment. The original unmet demand can later recover that stock through another real carry. Storage/filter edits preserve all physical contents and queue relocation for disallowed/excess stock, including multiple portions of the same type. Repeated restrictive edits extend an existing outstanding relocation while preserving its age/identity; physical source quantities already include unpicked claims and exclude carried quantities, and prior deposits remain counted once. Physical constraints are reconciled at promise/handling boundaries, before new work is selected. An outgoing pickup promise can temporarily cover excess, but its cancellation/invalidation restores the still-required relocation. The original relocation retains its identity/age until the source physically meets its constraint, even if its initial smaller portion has already deposited. Access-only edits retain existing filters. Blocked relocation remains demand rather than deleting freight or spawning overflow inventory.

## Bounded A06 T01 yard case

An actual eligible P08 reports to the stationary owned hull, completes three seconds of station handling and occupies its one Driver station as a physical carrier occupant. An unqualified person, remote person, active carry or supplier crew cannot substitute. One person cannot carry and drive simultaneously. Independent movement of an occupied driver is refused by the ledger.

One declared clear route uses straight T01 road speed 12 m/s, 10 m rolling-turn radius and arcs limited to 45 degrees/s (7.854 m/s). It travels 177.124 m in 16.833 seconds, consumes distance × 0.002 fuel, and requires fuel with a 25% reserve before starting. The fixed path runs from F11 at (180,140), north to (180,70), around a 10 m semicircle, south to (160,130), then around a 10 m quarter turn into parking at (170,140). It uses the 7×2.7 m T01 footprint and declared clear lane/turn area. This is one bounded yard move, not dispatch, strategic route planning, refuelling, a five-hull catalogue or a produced-vehicle collection system.

The one F11 fixture has four finite parking places; this route reserves its second place before leaving its first. Occupied/reserved destination parking refuses movement. The moving driver keeps actual yard duty/station and pending new duty. Only physical arrival converts reserved parking to occupied; three seconds of stationary exit then vacates the station and applies the pending duty. A later qualified yard assignment reports to the hull's actual parked position and repeats stationary entry there, preserving fuel/distance and occupied parking. It does not create a second trip. Cancelling approach or unfinished station entry cannot later seat a reassigned driver. An empty uncrewed hull cannot repeat the move. The full G07 station/capability catalogue and all battlefield/fleet rules remain separate.
