# G14 HQ issue boundary

The shared identity ledger owns each HQ stock stack, weapon, person, hull, fuel unit, and reservation. Dispatch unloads manifests through its existing berth handling into `hq-stock` and `hq-reserve`; arrival or an issue claim leaves recipients unchanged. The HQ service only reserves owned stock already in `hq-stock` and issues it after a named local handler's timed pickup and drop. Reserve personnel walk to stock and back before their issue job completes. Vehicle ammunition must match the same stopped military hull's rack capacity at the HQ berth, while M08 fuel enters only its actual tank. Cancelled active carries return physically to HQ stock before releasing the remaining claim.

HQ command automatically selects admitted, stopped military hulls at the berth for matching ammunition and fuel, then trained generic HQ Reserve people for a minimum E01 rifle and 30 E02 rounds. It finishes a recipient's matching rounds before promising another rifle and does not count a mere incoming passenger or an untrained/specialist reserve as an eligible generic rifle recipient. This bounded policy supplies physical issue without claiming G15's squad organization, specialist roles, mission priorities, or remote supply decisions.

The four logistics staff among the five fixed HQ people are exclusive across dispatch unloading and issue jobs. The fifth remains the Commander. HQ stock remains limited to 20,000 kg and the reserve to 96 people; the two receiving berths and military fleet/population admissions still belong to dispatch. Dispatch inspection shows local available and reserved whole quantities, intact and promised weapon counts, active issue progress, and the next HQ command shortage. A manual HQ Diagnostics tab is available only when `TRENCHO_HQ_DIAGNOSTICS=1` for development tests; normal play does not offer player issue commands.

A24 is an explicit boundary: unloading E25 shells at HQ cannot refill a T05 two kilometres away. Only the same tank at the HQ berth may receive a local rack handoff. Remote delivery and local battlefield transfer are G23/G26, and save/restore is G32.

G05 integration keeps construction and HQ issue as separate owners on the same rear ledger and event clock. Both contribute their next boundary, advance, settle, snapshot, and audit without transferring one system's stock or people into the other. The normal Build tab follows Dispatch; optional HQ Diagnostics follows Build. A concurrent regression runs a real F03 build and local T05 shell issue together, and the rendered input cases cover both tabs after this ordering change.
