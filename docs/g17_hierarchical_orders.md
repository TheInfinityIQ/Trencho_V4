# G17 hierarchical orders and information

G17 uses the same scene-owned rear clock and actual G01 identities as G15 and G16. An active dismounted squad samples G08 sight through its active members once per second. It shares the original observed position and time after 0.5 seconds, then reports climb squad–platoon–company–Commander links at one second each. A stale report keeps its original time: confidence falls by 0.02 per second, position uncertainty grows 2 m per second, and strength estimates gain 25% caution after ten seconds. At zero confidence there is no targetable position. These time and display coefficients are reversible implementation choices for the G17 information delay; they do not confer hidden exact enemy state.

Orders carry action, outcome, purpose, constraints, priority and fallback one real link at a time. A subordinate receives after one second and acknowledges after another. An acknowledged intent can be relayed to a real child, retaining its parent ID. Ordinary higher-level mission changes wait ten seconds and squad tactical changes wait three; Emergency safety changes may interrupt. Progress and inability reports retain their original observation time through the upward links. The review service has no autonomous movement or omniscient inventory access.

The supported-flank decision uses a delivered current hostile contact, real G15 squad capability and leader positions, G16 eligible fixing fire with loaded matching rounds, two distinct platoon squads, at least 30 degrees of actual separation, and at least 1.3 times the known enemy strength. The fixing squad holds the fire line; the flanking squad advances only while support, contact and cohesion remain. Loss of fixing ammunition pauses the flank, reports the local shortage and escalates unresolved inability after ten seconds. Ordered squads whose leader remains absent for twenty seconds hold and report an inability. G15 may provide a replacement sooner.

Enemy HQ registration declares a physical scenario marker and supplies no contact by itself. Prepare Assault records actual person-level E02 shortages and an operation plan's route, transport, fuel and engineering requirements without granting goods. The route owner supplies feasible supply/fallback evidence and per-operating-hull route-and-fallback fuel estimates; G17 adds the GDD's 25% reserve and compares the requirement with the same assigned hulls' physical M08 tanks. A supplied M08 source ID is inspected for available and reserved units; absent or incompatible sources stay explicitly unassigned. A foot approach or clear engineering survey needs explicit evidence. Unknown prerequisites remain pending, named vehicle transport must be assigned, crewed and boarded, and a required G21 project remains blocked until completed. Reassessment refreshes shortages from the physical ledger. Final eligibility cannot skip preparation and requires a delivered HQ location, feasible route, no unresolved preparation demand, 0.75 company readiness, ninety actual E02 rounds per rifle/marksman carrier, operating armoured or indirect support named in the plan with actual movement capability, and 1.5 local superiority over delivered defender estimates. An apparently empty HQ needs a current reconnaissance sighting. Route planning, G21 demolition, G23 mounted combat, G27 care and G32 save/restore remain their respective feature owners; G17 exposes its state in `snapshot()` for future persistence.

The G17 review scene is a controlled fixture. Its buttons observe with real members, advance the shared clock, assign distinct fixing/flanking responsibilities, physically expend fixing rounds, and place a physical sight obstruction. It displays report age, confidence, uncertainty, order state, escalation and the physical audit. A28 and A29 focused regressions verify continuous refresh, delayed observation, sight loss, ammunition loss, pause and escalation. A separate regression verifies all downward and upward command links and §16.3 blocked gates.

G17 owns observation assignments, last delivered contacts, in-transit link messages, intent, local responsibility, failure timers and preparation demands. Inspection is detached from the physical ledger. Ending observation clears live visibility but retains aging reports; a failed support role pauses rather than deleting the parent order or transferring its goods. The current command APIs do not cancel committed physical movement. G32 must serialize the remaining delivery times, original observation times, leader/failure timers, intent lineage and objective markers alongside G01/G15/G16 physical state, then rebind the scene-owned service to the restored shared clock. The review lists actual member assignments and positions, loaded rounds, loose available/reserved E02, pending messages and actionable blockers so it can be reconciled against the ledger.
