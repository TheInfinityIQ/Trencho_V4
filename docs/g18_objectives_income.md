# G18 objectives and income

G18 owns a scene-local set of physical objective places, control values, ownership transitions and objective-income totals on the accepted rear clock. A scenario may register up to six Resource sites, two Route Hubs and one Observation site. Registration requires a real G01 place and creates neither people nor goods. Every site has a 25 m radius and control from enemy −60 to player +60.

At each event boundary, the service reads actual G01 people, holders, carried weapons and matching available or loaded rounds, and G16 Broken status. Only living, unbroken, armed dismounted people inside the radius count, capped at four work per second per army. G15 vehicle passengers and crew have a carrier holder and contribute zero even when the hull is parked on the objective. Both armies present freeze control and resource income. With nobody present, partial control returns at two per second toward the current owner's end or toward zero while unowned. Ownership is lost at zero and gained at the full end. Participants in a new full capture gain five morale through the G01 physical person record.

The existing G02 requisition account retains its baseline six per second. G18 credits 1.5 per second from each player-owned uncontested Resource site directly into that same money and income accounting; Route Hubs and Observation sites provide no money. Boundaries at zero and ±60 split elapsed time so income begins and ends at the actual ownership instant. Control never manufactures fuel, materials or depot stock. The service exposes a detached inspection of progress, current eligible people, physical positions, blockers, owner transitions, income rate and accrued income. G19 and later route/observation systems can consume ownership without treating a Route Hub as a teleporting supply permission; G18 itself declares no HQ victory.

The controlled review uses a real four-person G15 squad aboard an admitted, crewed T02. This is the issue's staged passenger-occupancy case. The literal operational APC A30 is repeated in G23/G25 as specified by issue #26. The buttons dismount those same people, advance time, introduce a real armed opposing person and physically retreat; they do not place or command ordinary combat forces in the main game. The 960×540 render lists passenger and crew IDs, assignments, positions, loaded and available/reserved rounds, progress, income and blocked reasons.

G18 owns no separate people, weapon, stock or currency ledger. Its save/restore state is the objective registry, physical place IDs, control, owner, accrued income and dated ownership events; G32 must restore it with the G01 positions/holders, G16 morale and G02 money at the same paused clock. Interrupting capture leaves the earned control value, subject to the documented uncontested or empty decay. There is no speculative cancellation that refunds capture work or awards income early. Focused regressions cover occupied transport, dismount, neutral timing, contest, reversal, decay, physical eligibility and requisition reconciliation.
