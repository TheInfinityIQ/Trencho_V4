# G12 connected dispatch manifests

[Issue 20](https://github.com/TheInfinityIQ/Trencho_V4/issues/20) implements the bounded GDD 7, 9, 10 and 23.6 dispatch contract on the shared rear clock and identity ledger. Each accepted manifest names one existing F10-produced, P08-driven, fuelled, parked hull. Passenger commitments name existing rear people; cargo names actual local stock or intact weapons. Acceptance reserves military people and, for Fleet Transfer, the incoming military hull slot before physical loading. Requested, committed, staged, onboard, arrived and unloaded values remain separate in the Dispatch view.

The same hull drives from F11 to F07, workers carry stock and weapons, and passengers report and board one at a time. An E13 must occupy the real F10 bay; two actual Vehicle Works loaders spend 20 seconds handling it and carry that same object directly onto the hull. T02 uses eight passenger seats when carrying a gun. A20's eight people, eight rifles and 480 rounds use 681.6 kg; A60's nine people plus gun is rejected before reserving claims. Fuel is consumed across the bounded rear exit, friendly entry and 400 m HQ leg. At HQ, actual passenger exits and reserve arrivals, freight unload, and Fleet Transfer hull/crew handoff convert admissions to occupancy. Strategic crew/hull retain rear places and return to the same F11 slot. A predeparture cancellation withdraws only its own commitments; physical passengers, goods and guns still unload before the hull parks.

The fixed HQ fixture has five staff, a 96-person reserve, two receiving berths and 20,000 kg stock. A third hull waits at the outside HQ queue until a berth claim becomes free, then docks physically. Military people and hull admissions are limited to 384 and 24; returning crew retain rear home capacity. A pre-HQ safety return keeps the same hull, occupants and incoming military claims outside while obtaining rear readmission for nonreturners. A full rear refuses readmission. The claims convert only at actual rear entry and hull receipt; physical unloading and parking follow. [G13](routes_fuel_load.md) adds measured route and fuel decisions to this lifecycle, with specified bridge-class fixtures; G20 owns built-bridge traversal. G32 must persist manifest claims, object locations, handling/route progress, HQ admissions, return holds and the shared clock without replaying a receipt or release.

Verification: `game/dispatch/tests/dispatch_regression.gd` covers A20/A21/A60, physical loading, HQ receipt, cancellation and mode edits. `game/dispatch/tests/dispatch_input_test.gd` drives the normal entry Dispatch controls and captures representative 1280×720 and 960×540 views in display CI. Windows native input remains under the existing platform gate.
