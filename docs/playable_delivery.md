# First connected playable delivery

Authorized transition 30 October 2026. Live tracking: [#83](https://github.com/TheInfinityIQ/Trencho_V4/issues/83).

## Experience and scope
Operate the rear, manufacture supplies, dispatch real transport and observe autonomous battlefield capability change when supplies arrive or are withheld. Repeat actual resupply in the same continuing session.
Keep full 4,000 by 6,000 metre scale, physical identity/conservation, hierarchy and autonomous military control. Start with one declared representative full-scale scenario. Other map families, campaign, persistence and remaining roster remain final requirements. No hidden replenishment, diagnostic resets, teleportation or weakened assault eligibility.
The authorized transition adopts clear 2D overhead and Windows-first for this build, at existing 1280x720 development viewport. This permits connected-world implementation; D01 hardware/performance/native certification remains open and is not claimed.
Designer records initial scenario/forces/resources and pacing checkpoints on #83 before dependent implementation. GDD proposed defaults remain baseline. Material deviations need explicit design resolution and human approval where they change agreed experience. The full engagement 30–50 simulated-minute target is a playtest objective, not a passed criterion or forced demo timeout.

## Observable acceptance
- Ordinary launch enters one persistent session. Rear/battlefield share time and identities; view switching does not pause/reset the other.
- Acquisition, staffing, manufacture, loading, travel, unloading, actual issue and consumption connect through real entities.
- Needed supply and withholding produce observable capability differences and understandable shortages.
- A second physical resupply cycle works; play continues.
- A meaningful contested battlefield outcome and economic/logistical consequence is observable under existing rules.
- Inspection explains shortages and useful player actions. Player manages logistics; military AI controls tactics.
- Lead delivers exact build, launch instructions/controls and brief direct verification of changed behavior under [current verification policy](current_change_verification.md). Broader regression and integrated testing is deferred until the main game is complete.
- Designer reviews fidelity, clarity/pacing; human feedback remains distinct from engineering acceptance.
