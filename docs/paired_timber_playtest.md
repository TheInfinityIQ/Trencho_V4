# Lower-tier timber playtest checkpoint

Order up to **50 kg Timber** in the normal 3D game. The four existing apron
workers provide the pair; keep two eligible for unloading. Watch the real truck
open, the same two workers prepare storage and collect one bundle, carry it down
the ramp and into a reachable lower warehouse cell. Its receipt permits safe
truck securing/departure and another supported order.

Orders needing upper truck handling are refused before payment. Quantities,
fees, truck packing, original fixtures and approved S4 motion are preserved.
A held truck displays its storage/handling reason. Do not order another timber
load once reachable lower storage is full; upper and onward handling are unfinished.

Use **F5** on the delivered separate project copy. It starts in 3D at **1x**.
**Tab** opens management; Pause / 1x / 2x use the shared time controls.
This checkpoint has not been launched locally by the developer.

imported pose alignment, logical pause/resume and next purchase with actual
