# Animation approval

Dawson requests a complete video whenever an animation is ready. Each delivered
version must show the full action, including its ending and settling. Looping
clips show the loop boundary. Record the clip names, source asset hashes,
durations and whether playback shows the Artist asset or actual Godot integration.
Stills support appearance review; they do not establish full motion approval.

The current worker and soldier clips, construction smoke/cut/tape/pylon sequence
and personal pickup motion need videos under this convention. The Artist owns
asset playback reels. The Lead records the complete integrated construction and
driving sequence. A driving preview includes starting, both turns, stopping and
settling. Provisional integration may continue, but Dawson's animation approval
remains **pending** until he explicitly approves the displayed version.

Offline fixed-frame recording makes motion review reproducible. It does not
demonstrate real-time frame pacing, native controls or60FPS. Use the existing
brief remote current-change workflow; this convention does not authorize local
engine runs or regression suites. Video capture setup stays out of normalF5.

Source: [Godot Movie Maker](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/animation/creating_movies.html)
explicitly distinguishes offline motion rendering from real-time gameplay capture.
