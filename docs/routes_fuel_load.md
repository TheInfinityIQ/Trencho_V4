# G13 measured routes, fuel and gross load

Issue [#21](https://github.com/TheInfinityIQ/Trencho_V4/issues/21) implements the GDD 9.2, 13.3, 14 and 23.1 route contract on the accepted G12 manifest and G07 produced hull. The same ledger owns the vehicle, every occupant and carried item, fractional tank fuel, and the dispatch receipt. Route selection creates no second inventory or instantaneous refuel.

The bounded rear road is measured from the parked hull through the rear exit, 1,000 m off-map road, 400 m HQ approach and physical HQ berth. A Strategic Delivery also measures the return to its promised F11 place. Fleet Transfer plans one way to HQ, its next resupply. Each segment uses the vehicle's canonical speed and fuel rate, terrain movement and fuel factors, slope rules, actual distance, and 25 percent fuel margin. A damaged hull below one-quarter health is immobile; at or below half health its route speed is reduced. Departure reads only physically transferred M08 in the tank. A failed fuel guard leaves the loaded manifest, occupants and goods at the dispatch berth. A later real whole-unit carry can fill that same tank and retry.

The route planner validates finite endpoints, a whole-hull clear width, rolling turn clearance, deep water, unsupported fords, forbidden slopes, known mines and observed hostile fire. It can also read an authored `TerrainWorld` lane: every swept footprint cell contributes support, speed and fuel, and solid obstacles reject the route. The current rear/HQ road is a declared bounded corridor with one selected surface; no generated battlefield route or map service exists yet. Unsafe forward observations stop the same travelling hull for ten seconds; clearing resumes its original measured route, while expiry requests physical safety return with rear space and measured fuel. A failed return leaves the hull and claims stranded, visible for recovery.

Gross bridge load reads the actual hull mass, nested occupants/gear/freight/ammunition and tank fuel from the authoritative ledger. Specified B01/B02/B03 fixtures apply width, span and load limits before departure. The crossing measures 20 m holding approaches and at most 4 m/s across the bridge section. A23 tests fully occupied and loaded T04/T05 hulls: B02 rejects their actual gross, B03 accepts within its real 80,000 kg and width limits. These are specified class fixtures; G20 will connect the decision to engineer-built bridge state, traffic, damage and actual crossing traversal under GDD 23.1. Towing and coupled gross loads remain with the later recovery/combat work.

The dispatch review shows route surface, crossing class, clear width, measured distance/time, fuel plus reserve, tank contents, gross load and blocker. The focused G13 regression proves mixed authored terrain, swept obstacle rejection, B02/B03 load decisions, A22 rejection and physical refuel retry, distinct one-way/return requirements, safety hold/resume/expiry, and conservation. `tools/check.ps1` runs it with the existing full checks; the scene input suite exercises the new controls at normal and small window sizes. Headless tests do not establish visual readability by themselves.

G12 cancellation still owns predeparture claim release and physical unload; G13 changes no cancellation transaction. A travelling hazard interrupts motion at its recorded position, fuel and measured segment; clearing it continues the same segment, and safety return retains people/goods and military admissions until actual rear readmission. G32 save/restore must persist the selected profile, measured sections, route start/progress/burn, stop deadline and reason, tank contents, manifest mode and admissions, without replaying a departure, fuel transfer, receipt or return release. No save/restore implementation is claimed by G13.
