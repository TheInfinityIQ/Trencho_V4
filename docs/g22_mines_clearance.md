# G22 mines, IEDs and clearance

`game/mines/mine_service.gd` owns D01–D03 projects, ground scanning and disarm tasks, observed hazard markers and device events on the existing rear clock. G01 owns people, kits, physical M06, exclusive stock claims, receipts and hull health. D04 remains owned by G21; its devices participate in the same scan/disarm and combined squad remote reservation. No manufactured mine item, explosive recovery or sympathetic explosion is introduced.

## Physical actions and knowledge

An active Engineer squad assigns one actual available P02 with E08. Completed placement consumes the exclusively claimed nearby M06 batch once, after 8/12/20 active seconds for D01/D02/D03. Moving away, loss of kit or unsafe observed hostiles pauses work and retains unconsumed stock. Cancellation before completion releases claims; it does not teleport stock. The planted device has 20 health and arms after ten seconds. Placement reservations enforce four metres separation and count toward the army's 96 pressure-device limit. D03 reserves the squad's same D03/D04 remote slot throughout placement and its planted lifetime. Terminal devices release that reservation.

Actual ledger movement checks swept physical presence. D01 responds to a dismounted person, including a friendly; D02 responds only to a moving vehicle whose oriented footprint enters the 1.5-metre trigger circle. A coarse movement stops at first contact before damage. D01 also tests dismounted presence when arming completes. D03 needs a living squad P02/E08 controller within 300 metres, a currently observed hostile vehicle in its six-metre trigger zone, and no living friendly body or hull within the six-metre blast plus five-metre margin. Operator loss requires explicit takeover.

The Designer confirmed on 30 October 2026: the actual D02 trigger hull takes direct 650 damage at bottom penetration 160; D03's assigned observed trigger hull takes direct 900 at bottom penetration 180. Activation radius determines contact, rather than attenuating this direct hit. Other entities take linear blast falloff using closest footprint distance and applicable cover. The direct and blast results never stack. Any surviving qualifying device hull hit sets Damage Immobilized. The accepted G23 surface-damage helper is copied unchanged onto the G21 source base, with a Bottom wrapper. During G24 integration, a connected synchronous `vehicle_damaged` owner suppresses the compatibility occupant fallback. External blast snapshots exclude seated occupants before hull resolution, preventing a destroying explosion from injuring the same ejected people twice. G24 owns final destruction/salvage/recovery; this branch does not claim that later issue's completion.

Only an actual ground observer of placement, a stationary equipped P02 scan for three seconds within twenty metres with clear ground sight, or witnessed detonation gives the opposing side a marker. A marker includes class, position and observation time, never unseen stock or health. Discovery or a witnessed trigger excludes the surrounding thirty metres from ordinary routes. G13 route planning uses the requesting hull owner's received knowledge; active G12 outbound convoys stop on newly received hazards using their existing ten-second hold/alternative-return behavior. A timer or manual resume cannot authorize the known unsafe route. Unknown devices still act physically. A surviving mine-struck convoy reports Stranded rather than completing its route. Physical collision prediction limits the travelled movement portion and burns its fuel before synchronous device damage; G24 can then lose the remaining fuel once on destruction.

A revealed D01–D04 takes twelve active seconds to disarm with actual P02/E08 within two metres. Interruption preserves earned work. Deliberate positive damage to a currently observed revealed device only makes it inert after twenty cumulative damage; no blast or recovered M06 occurs. A cleared device's routes update for the observing side only. Another side must observe clearance or scan it. A witnessed spent device remains a hazard marker until actual observation/sweeping confirms clearance.

## Command context and limits

`order` accepts an optional supplied plan. It records an actually observed approach vehicle's class/position/source/time, marked friendly corridor and retreat-route points, and rejects a site within thirty metres of either supplied passage. Inspection exposes these consequences. The existing engineer-squad service architecture is retained by Designer direction: a global autonomous placement/route-selection planner is not added here. Final-world useful threatened-approach selection and corridor/retreat decisions remain explicitly with G10 #18 and G17 #25 command/world integration. The review fixture supplies a visible opposing approach and a marked corridor/retreat alternative; it does not claim a generated battlefield or player mine-placement control.

`game/mines/mine_demo.tscn` is available through the existing G22 review selector. It is a bounded, declared flat-road review at the single development resolution 1280×720. Its controls exercise A37 physical delivery/work/arming/footprint damage and A38 scan/disarm/received knowledge. The starting Engineer squad, opposing T05 with an actual P08 driver, and three M06 are declared fixture resources; actual downstream actions use the shared services. It is not production presentation.

G32 #40 must save project identities, position, fixed work and earned progress, M06 claims/receipts, device health/state and arming deadline, controller and shared remote reservation, side-specific markers/observation times, scan/disarm progress/stationary point, convoy safety holds, immobilization, and the shared event clock. Restore while paused and never replay consumption or a detonation. No save format is introduced here.
