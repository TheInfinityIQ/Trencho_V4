Trencho V4 - Empty-field opening candidate

Keep TrenchoDemo.exe and TrenchoDemo.pck together. Run TrenchoDemo.exe;
the Godot editor is not required. REVISION.txt identifies this build.

You start paused on a rear field with a blue player character, 2500 requisition,
500 Building Materials at the edge Supply Table, and no hired workers.
Use 1x to start time. WASD moves the character; Pause stops movement and time.
The mouse wheel zooms; middle-button drag pans; Fit base resets the camera.

First workshop:
1. Open Supplies or Workers / Jobs. Hire eight workers for 140 requisition.
   Their transport takes 90 seconds, followed by actual disembarkation.
   Purchased workers are not available immediately. The supplier driver leaves.
2. Once the eight workers have joined the pool, open Workers / Jobs and request
   four Hauling workers. Counts distinguish requested, assigned and present.
3. Open Build, choose Workshop, and move the preview onto clear field space.
   A location near the upper-left field, around 60 metres east / 45 south,
   is suitable. Click to place; R rotates; Esc or right-click cancels the draft.
   A red preview explains why placement is blocked. Preview/cancel spends nothing.
4. In Workers / Jobs request four Builders for the newly placed site.
   The site automatically requests its real Building Materials. Haulers carry
   them from the Supply Table; the builders must walk to the site and work.
5. The completed workshop consumes 150 Building Materials. Its builders are
   released, and selecting the workshop opens its production and staffing panel.

Supplies also lists purchasable raw resources with the full price and transport
fee. The resource display distinguishes local stock, reservations and deliveries.
More panels exposes the retained detailed controls.

This candidate demonstrates the corrected opening and first construction.
Routine production hauling and automatic Logistics Centre-to-HQ shipping are
still being connected. Army supply remains the earlier manual prototype.
Manufacturing, repeated construction, onward logistics and full-game completion
are not certified by the opening check. Battlefield retains the authored forces
and shares the same clock; switching views does not start a new game.

Use New game only to replace the current session. Start at 1x. The project retains
30 FPS and four worker-thread limits; full Windows responsiveness is unverified.
