# Construction capacity and personal pickup

Accepted gameplay direction, 9 November 2026. Dawson requested a maximum of two construction workers without an office, four additional places per completed Construction Office, a player-drivable faceted pickup with visible sway, and full videos of ready animations for his approval. These rules describe the intended experience; implementation and player acceptance remain separate.

The first slice keeps base building and logistics central. Construction capacity makes offices useful as the workforce grows. The personal pickup lets the same manager travel around the rear without taking a vehicle or worker away from automatic logistics.

## Shared construction capacity

The whole base has **2 + 4 × completed operational Construction Offices** construction-worker places. With no office, capacity is two; one office allows six, and two allow ten. An unfinished office grants no capacity. Completing an office grants capacity even if its staffing request is unfilled; it does not create workers.

Construction staffing commitments share this limit across all sites and office builder pools. Travel, pending reporting and fetching materials for the assigned site still occupy the worker's construction place. Moving the same worker between an office pool and a site does not count that person twice. Targets and individual reassignment must respect the shared limit, with used and total capacity visible before confirmation. The existing maximum of four builders working at one site remains.

The Construction Office's starting request remains two actual workers from the shared pool. These are construction workers available for site work, not a new administrative profession. Their office membership and site deployment represent the same people. A capacity increase neither grants four people nor fills every available place automatically. The manager is not one of the hired builders.

Workers can start the first office using the two base construction places and actual materials. Assigned builders retain their accepted ability to fetch missing materials for their own site. A fetching, carrying or reporting worker cannot simultaneously earn construction work.

Before starting office dismantling, compare construction commitments with the capacity that would remain without that office. Include the dismantling crew. Reject an over-capacity request and explain how many construction places must be released first. Preserve people, active carries and the player's other assignments; do not choose workers to dismiss automatically. Existing access, goods-clearance and safe-handoff requirements still apply.

## Personal development pickup

Provide one personal pickup at a clear starting roadside position in the rear, outside the supplier stopping area and walking access. This is an explicit development-opening addition. It is separate from Construction Office utility trucks, supplier carriers and army transport, with no goods/passenger service or cargo-transfer role in this slice.

The Artist creates original faceted blue pickup art inspired by Dawson's [1980 Toyota Hilux reference](https://www.classic.com/veh/1980-toyota-hilux-rn37023005-4y86xop/). The goal is a coherent small working pickup in Trencho's style, rather than an exact reproduction. The Artist owns the art and animation delivery; the Lead owns driving integration.

Press **E** near the stopped pickup to enter it. The same manager occupies the driver's seat; entering does not create a driver or leave a second controllable manager outside. The personal pickup requires no hired worker or military driving qualification. Press E to exit when stopped and adjacent ground is clear. If there is no valid exit, keep the manager seated and explain why.

Use **W/S** to move forward or reverse, **A/D** to steer and **Space** to brake. Show these controls when driving. Start with a readable third-person chase view. First-person driving may follow if it needs additional camera work; the manager's existing walking views remain available after exit.

Turning produces restrained side-to-side body roll. Starting and stopping produce fore-and-aft pitch that settles smoothly. The pickup's actual movement must remain controllable and respect the rear's ground and solid building boundaries. Visual sway must not create extra movement, inventory or work. This slice does not add combat, crushing, damage, racing or access to military/supplier vehicles.

Opening management or building controls, losing driving focus, or switching to the battlefield removes driving input and brakes the pickup to rest. Keep the manager in the same vehicle and preserve its actual location. Returning does not resume a held throttle automatically. Global Pause freezes movement and the visual response. The rest of the game retains its normal progression while time runs.

Fuel is **unmetered for this personal development pickup**. Do not charge, grant or consume fuel for it or display a working fuel gauge. Identify this exception in its information. Freight, Construction Office utility trucks and military vehicles retain their existing finite-fuel rules. A later personal fuel economy is a separate gameplay decision.

## Full animation videos

Every ready animation needs a clearly identified full playback video for Dawson's approval. Cover the complete action from its beginning through its finish and settling; show repeatable clips looping cleanly. Stills or a cropped middle section do not replace the video.

Provide the existing ready worker and soldier clips and the full construction completion sequence as well as newly ready animations. Driving footage should show acceleration, braking, left and right turns, and the return to rest. Asset-only previews and integrated game footage must be labelled so they do not imply the same readiness.

Record animation acceptance as pending until Dawson approves the presented version. Implementation and provisional integration can continue; the request is not a blanket development pause or approval of every commit. Preserve the existing restrictions on local engine runs and regression work when preparing playback evidence.

## Responsibility and scope

These rules supersede the older no-player-driving exclusion for the personal pickup and the unrestricted four-builders-per-site opening. They do not change military command ownership, automatic supply fulfilment, prices, recipes or battlefield starting stock. The Lead chooses technical architecture, handling values and the brief checks needed for the changed code. This note introduces no new engine, physics framework, traffic simulation or test campaign requirement.
