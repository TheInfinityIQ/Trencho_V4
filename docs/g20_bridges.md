# G20 bridge construction and traversal

GDD sections 13.1–13.4 and issue #28 govern this bounded integration. `game/bridges/bridge_service.gd` owns B01–B03 projects, repairs and traffic on the existing rear clock, G01 ledger and G08 terrain world. The project does not create a second inventory, transport scheduler or autonomous engineer planner.

An order checks two stable land bank cells, a contiguous sampled TR11 water gap, maximum clear span, a real metre-cell deck, and the six crossing cap. Both banks have physical supply/repair places. The chosen bank stages exactly the full blueprint M11 before two to four local P02 with actual E08 may start. Four engineers earn four work seconds per simulation second. Visible hostile proximity or recent site damage pauses work. B03 across 30 clear metres consumes 2,400 M11 once, earns 1,200 work seconds, and completes at 7,000 health. The controlled carrier arrival in the review is a physical endpoint fixture; G26a owns live forward hauling and unloading.

Completion adds deck support to the authored world. Whole footprint, movement class, actual nested vehicle gross, bridge width, live health/load, and twenty metre approach speed feed the G13 route check. `request_crossing` admits an entity only at a real holding point with sufficient fuel; the shared clock moves the same identity and burns actual tank fuel. One vehicle or one coupled T05 tow occupies a bridge. B01 batches up to eight actual people and checks their combined body plus gear mass against the current 1,000 kg limit, halved when damaged. Opposing queues wait, and a visible hostile at the far approach or a reported known mine blocks ordinary admission. G21 will supply actual mine detection and explosive denial; G24 owns broader vehicle recovery and tow eligibility.

Damage at or below half health halves load and caps new crossing speed at 2 m/s. Destruction removes the whole deck and kills actual people and vehicle occupants still over the water; already crossing loads do not trigger collapse merely from crossing a newly reduced threshold. Repair consumes 10 local M11 and 40 engineer-work per capped 100 health. Full rebuild needs the original blueprint quantity and work from either bank. Sound empty B02 may close for a B03 upgrade with only the full-cost difference; cancellation reopens B02 and recovers half of any consumed upgrade M11 as a real pile.

`game/bridges/tests/bridge_regression.gd` covers A23, A33, A34, A35, staged material, interruption, live traffic, fuel, repair, upgrade, rebuild, collapse and accounting. `bridge_demo.tscn` provides actual button input and a metre diagram for a 30 m B03; its controlled arrival and review positions are declared fixtures. The input test stores a 960×540 render under ignored `.artifacts/g20/`. G32 still owns save/restore of bridge projects, queue/traffic, changed world support, stock and exact progress.
