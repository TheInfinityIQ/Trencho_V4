# G23 vehicle armour and weapons

G23 connects T03 roof W02, T04 W06, and T05 W07/coax W02 to the same manufactured hulls, people, E02/E25/E26 stacks, terrain observation, and simulation clock used by G07/G14/G15/G16/G18. The physical rack is the source of ammunition. A committed reload merely changes a mount's loaded magazine count; one actual unreserved hull-held round is consumed at each release. HQ stock, truck freight, and reservations never count as local firing stock. A T04's crew stations remain distinct from its eight passenger seats; actual dismount through military organization precedes objective eligibility.

## Ownership and decisions

- IdentityLedger owns hull health, its stored facing, stocked rounds, crew identity and positions. A timed movement update turns the hull toward its actual travel direction; a stationary turn can set facing explicitly. `apply_vehicle_surface_damage` checks Front/Side/Rear/Top/Bottom armour before changing health. Front and rear each cover 60 degrees from the respective facing. G21's separate blast-top damage boundary on its isolated branch should be consolidated with this operation at ordered integration, keeping both caller contracts and tests.
- VehicleCombatService owns turret aim, one-second T05 weapon switches, magazine counts, reload deadlines, W06 burst phase, firing deadlines, in-flight cannon impacts and its event history. Only a living qualified actual Gunner can fire; W07 additionally requires the actual Loader. A Driver is required for moving fire, while an otherwise undercrewed stationary gun can defend. Main fire against exposed infantry requires four in the six-metre cluster and a safe blast area.
- G07's VehicleActivity readiness remains the rear registered-hull view, where crew have rear duties. The same hull's HQ military crew use formation duties after transfer, so G23 applies the same Gunner plus Loader plus local E25 rule from the ledger identities instead of calling that rear-only view. A driver alone still supports transfer, while ordinary combat deployment waits for all stations.
- The G16 personal-fire boundary keeps personal weapons and people. G23 extends target eligibility to actual vehicles and exposed T03 gunners. Direct mounted fire checks world sight and friendly line obstruction, then uses the struck hull surface for penetration. W06 and W07 rounds travel at 300 m/s, minimum 0.25 seconds. Direct and blast damage from a hit are combined as one maximum valid injury per target. An intact armoured hull shields its seated people from surrounding blast; a penetrating cannon hit of at least 35 hull damage causes each occupant one ten-injury event.
- Completed G19 structures and G20 bridges are resolved through their owning services before the matching ledger place is considered. Their own damage operations change structure health at projectile impact. A structure row is never passed to the vehicle armour operation.
- W02 has a 60-round magazine and 0.25-second cadence; W06 has 30 rounds and releases at 0, 0.33 and 0.67 seconds, with the following burst beginning at 2 seconds. W07 has one loaded shell and an eight-second cadence/reload with its Loader. Local reload takes six seconds for W02/W06 and may be interrupted without consuming the rack. A tank Loader can reload W07 while the Gunner operates the coax. Exact numbers are GDD v0.2 proposed defaults, not balance claims.

## Interruption and later integration

Lost crew or a destroyed hull blocks new releases. A round already fired remains in flight and resolves at its recorded impact position; moving a target out of that position prevents a direct hit but does not erase the blast. A direct-fire miss lands 1.5 blast radii away from the original target point in a seeded random direction, so it may still strike another physical entity near that point; it does not copy direct damage onto a moved target. Cancelling a reload clears its deadline and leaves all stock intact. Firing cannot be cancelled after the round is consumed. G24 owns full destruction aftermath, ejection, salvage, recovery and towing; G23 does not invent those systems. G26 owns forward resupply and cannot directly refill the rack from remote HQ stock.

G32 must persist the ledger's hull facing and all VehicleCombatService state: aim target/angle/deadline, selected T05 mount and switch deadline, magazine rounds, reload deadlines, next-fire deadlines, burst phase, pending projectiles, RNG state and event times. Restore deadlines relative to the same world clock and revalidate each pending projectile's physical target and position. The authoritative ledger already persists physical people, stocks, claims, health and carrier locations through G32's eventual snapshot. This feature currently has no save interface; a restart starts a fresh scenario rather than silently pretending to resume combat.

## Evidence boundary

The focused G23 regression uses actual manufactured T03–T05 hull identities, timed HQ crew reports, hull-held E02/E25/E26, remote and local HQ shell issue, struck front/rear/top/bottom, a cannon blast left at the original impact after its target moves, a constructed hostile S02 and B01 damaged after projectile flight, and real T04 passengers dismounting for objective control. Existing G14/G15/G16/G18 tests remain part of the full check. These controlled cases are not an AI fire-selection, forward movement or G24 wreck-playthrough claim.
