## Decisions and preserved constraints

Recommend the existing warehouse bale contact and the two metal cassette/service-window proposals as the concrete next art contracts. The600mm rail opening retains the entire tray and16 cell positions; it is a service-structure choice requiring a properly built revision with suitable ends/supports, not permission to leave a broken mesh. No original asset was modified and no structural load rating is claimed.

For S2/S4, freeze actual support/access geometry next. The local case handles now fit the actual glove, so inventing larger handles or a new pickup body is not supported by this evidence. The S4 bale obstruction is in the upper tray/lip, not the hand target. Do not silently remove load support, alter package yaw, accept overhang or reduce capacity to hide it. The reported clash envelope is specific to this retained body/contact and is not a theorem that every possible posture/layout fails.

The older **four-left S1 zero-support claim is withdrawn**: it applied the S2/S3/S4 placement rule to asymmetric S1. The corrected side/transform data above supersedes it. The old90→72 calculation remains only pure-rack geometric capacity when substituting timber heights; it does not determine quoted truck counts, payload grouping or fees. Mixed timber/metal binding and all integration remain Lead-owned.

## Contact method and evidence

Contacts come from BVH rays on real strap surfaces, metal-band undersides and exposed handle bars. Broad glove facets (~960mm² each) locate palms; an actual distal finger facet locates the overhand bar grip. Weighted wrist skin is re-evaluated and fitted, not treated as an invented rigid box. Each hand/face is measured separately. Original body/leg matrices remain unchanged; accepted contacts have no arm clamp, no glove/payload penetration and sub-micrometre facet-target errors. Forearms and both gloves are included in surroundings checks.

Warehouse occupied-stock bounds cover all15 other cells, not just empty shelves or other metal. Truck diagnostics retain its actual open/deployed body, same-rack packing dependencies, three same-family modules per side where they fit and full opposite stock. No warehouse stock-order workaround was introduced. Static parked-arm/hand approaches preserve the original bend body; no action was created, replaced or exported. Native visualPI must still be applied exactly once later.
