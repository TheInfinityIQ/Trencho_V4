## Decision and limits

Recommend reviewing this **S4 bale-service variant** as the concrete alternative to cutting the upper bearing tray. Approval must cover the empty-only hinge/preparation operation, new10mm guards, end seats/locks and the combined lower-bale insert. If those changes are not acceptable, retain S4 unchanged and leave the lower-bale contact unresolved; do not silently accept overhang or reduce capacity.

There has been no new pose sweep, rigid-mechanism model, fixture authoring, new pickup/hauling clip or integration. After acceptance, actual hinge/end hardware, deployment with all permitted neighbouring cargo, lower hand approach and unchanged loaded support need source verification before mesh authoring/export. S2 mounting is still unresolved and is not approved through this S4 proposal. Frozen timber and warehouse back-bale contact are untouched.
