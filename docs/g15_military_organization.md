# G15 military organization

The G15 service lives under `game/military/` and shares the rear simulation's event clock and G01 identity ledger. It records companies, platoons, squads and vehicle attachments without creating replacement people or duplicate hull and equipment inventories. The existing dispatch and HQ services remain the admission and physical issue owners.

An ordinary squad reserves four to eight real HQ Reserve people, or six to eight for Artillery. Its type is checked against the GDD §11.2 minimum package and actual compatible issued items. A report intent keeps each person in HQ Reserve until the timed walk to the formation point completes; only then does their single duty change and their reserve place release. Existing squads get replacement places before new squads are considered. The command service receives distinct named needs from later mission systems, ordered by priority, blocked parent mission value and age. It requests actual specialist HQ issue jobs before it forms a squad. Generic Rifle issue remains with G14. The player sees organization and shortages on Dispatch and has no squad creation control.

Command can cancel an uncompleted squad, replacement or crew report. This releases only its pending roster or station intent and its own artillery claim; people, already issued goods and other squads' claims remain at their actual holders. A report that has already completed needs a real onward destination rather than a cancellation that erases the duty.

Artillery claims a specific unallocated E13 and ten unreserved local E14 before reporting. Four P05 and armed guards must report; the same gun and claimed shells transfer to the formation point before it becomes active. A second artillery squad cannot count those items. Other packages stay on their actual roster holders. Readiness is the minimum of person fraction, least satisfied desired equipment type and ammunition fraction; capability separately checks the ordinary minimum and a living appointed leader.

The hierarchy enforces three companies, three platoons per company, three infantry squads and two vehicle elements per platoon, two further service vehicles per company, eight roster places per squad, and 24 military hulls. The dispatch service continues to own the 384-person military admission, 96-place HQ Reserve admission and fleet handoff. Qualified actual reserve members report to distinct crew stations. Command attaches staffed hulls to available real parents. A mounted squad keeps its roster and occupies passenger seats separately from crew; it can leave at a lawful stopped place.

Vehicle readiness measures actual crew, health, matching rack stock and declared route fuel. A separate purpose-specific capability check prevents a numerically ready but empty or unfuelled hull from moving, firing or transporting a squad. A boarded squad remains a separate roster but cannot claim dismounted fire or capture capability until its actual exit.

Squad appointments select living trained members, with a Rifle acting exception. A lost leader gets a five-second delay before replacement; senior and acting Commander appointments reuse existing identities. The status snapshot exposes roster, parent, leader, readiness, crew and shortage reasons. G16 owns casualty state changes and combat, G17 owns orders and movement, G23 owns firing, and G32 owns persistence.
